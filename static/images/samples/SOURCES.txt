Sample photographs used by the disease-detection demo
=====================================================

Every file below is a real leaf photograph, not an illustration. Each one was
downloaded from Wikimedia Commons and is used here under the licence recorded
next to it. The detector's output for these images is what the on-screen
"correct about N% of the time" figures were measured from.

early_blight_leaf.jpg
  Diagnosis shown: Early Blight (tomato) - labelled "schematic diagram" in the UI
  This one is a drawn diagram, not a photograph. It is kept because concentric
  target lesions are much easier to see in a diagram than in a field photo, and
  because the detector's own photographs of early blight often come back
  inconclusive. Do not read its result as a real diagnosis.
