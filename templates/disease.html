💡 How to Take an Ideal Crop Photo
  • Focus on Individual Leaves: Capture a clear close-up of the affected leaf showing both symptomatic spots and green healthy tissue.
  • Natural Daylight: Take photos in daylight; avoid harsh shadows or direct blinding camera flash which washes out color balance.
  • Top & Underside Check: Diseases like Late Blight and Downy Mildew display white sporulation underneath the leaf. Check both sides.
  • Early Intervention: Diagnosing when lesions cover less than 15% of canopy increases recovery chances by over 90%.
🌿 Integrated Pest Management (IPM) Principle

Always prioritize organic bio-fungicides (e.g., Trichoderma, neem formulations) at initial symptom onset. Reserve synthetic chemical fungicides for rapid outbreak containment.
