How the score is built

46% how much of the measured skin is free of dark, pale, cut or off-colour patches, 30% ripeness and colour strength (green tissue counted against the lot only when the crop is not supposed to be green), and 24% how even the colour is across the pieces. A blurry or badly lit photo loses 8%. The price percentages are this app's working assumption about what a mandi pays per grade — they are not a quote from any market.
