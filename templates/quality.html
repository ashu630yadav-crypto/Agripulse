Grade A (Premium / Export Quality) +25% Price Premium

Score ≥ 85%. Pristine surface, vibrant color saturation, zero cuts or bruises. Demands highest value from modern retail & export houses.

Grade B (Fair Average Quality - FAQ) Mandi Benchmark

Score 65% - 84%. Minor non-damaging superficial marks, standard shape and size. Traded in bulk across wholesale APMC Mandis.

Grade C (Industrial / Processing) Factory Discount

Score < 65%. Significant cosmetic defects or over-ripeness. Best channeled immediately to canning, puree, juicing, or starch plants.
